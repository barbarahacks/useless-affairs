## ideas: stupid features

- **take a number:** before processing you get "you are no. 312,860". it counts down fast, then sticks at 2 until you click "i am still here".
- **hold music:** elevator muzak while you wait, cut in by "your request is important to us".
- **cookie banner:** "accept all" and "reject all" both lead to "your cookie preference has been rejected".
- **permission that expires:** the certificate's "valid for 4 minutes" is a live countdown. at zero it gets stamped expired: "please reapply".
- **appeal:** a refusal gets an appeal button. the same departments reject faster, and the next form is an appeal of the appeal.
- **in triplicate:** sometimes you must re-enter the request 3 times. the third box is upside down, and they have to match.
- **sign here:** draw a signature with the mouse. it gets rejected: "does not match the signature we made up for you".
- **out of office:** sometimes a department is on strike or on a coffee break. its desk is empty and the paper gets an auto-reply.
- **wrong department:** sometimes a desk says "this isn't our department" and the paper goes back to desk 1, once.
- **terms and conditions:** a huge scroll box. the agree checkbox only unlocks at the bottom, and the scroll bar keeps growing.
- **lunch break:** every few minutes the site closes for a 10-second lunch break and a "closed" sign covers the form.
- **progress bar:** goes to 99%, then backwards.
- **satisfaction survey:** any star rating is "rounded to 2.1. thank you."
- **stamp cursor:** click anywhere on the site to stamp "rejected" on the page itself.
- **permits wallet:** your certificates are kept on the device. all of them have expired.

### more ideas, dumber

- **office hours are real:** judging depends on your actual clock. before 10am nothing gets approved (no coffee yet). 12–1 the desks are empty. after 4:55pm everything is approved instantly: "we just want to go home".
- **closed on weekends:** on saturday and sunday the whole site is closed. the hackathon is on a weekend, so judges get a tiny "open anyway (judges only)" link.
- **dark mode by application:** the dark mode toggle is a request. you submit "can i have dark mode?", it goes through the departments, and if approved you get dark mode for 4 minutes.
- **the stamp runs out of ink:** each stamp is fainter than the last. by desk 7 it's barely visible: "ink requisition rejected".
- **rejection loyalty program:** "you have been rejected 12 times. you are now a gold member. gold members are rejected faster."
- **legalese:** your request is rewritten as a formal title. "can i blink" becomes "application for temporary voluntary ocular closure (form oc-1)". good for the claude version later.
- **receipt:** a thermal receipt prints out of the bottom of the page, far too long, itemising "1x hope ... $0.00" and "rejection handling fee ... $4.00".
- **mandatory meeting:** at random, every clerk walks to the middle of the floor, nothing happens for 10 seconds, and they walk back. "this meeting could have been an email."
- **unread memos:** the top bar shows "you have 4,019 unread memos", and it goes up every time a department cc's another one.
- **request password rules:** your request must contain a number, a department name and a feeling. it must not contain capital letters, which are not permitted on this website.
- **are you sure?** five in-page confirmations, each one more unsure. on the last one, yes and no swap places.
- **the submit button gets shy:** when the ministry of vibes is in a mood, the button slides away from your cursor.

### even more ideas

- **monotone voice:** each department reads its verdict out loud in a flat government voice (browser speech synthesis, slow and low, no assets).
- **ottawa news ticker:** a scrolling ticker of other citizens' cases. "kanata resident denied permission to sneeze." "permit to skate on the rideau canal under review since 1832."
- **o-train delay:** sometimes processing stops: "your request is delayed. it was sent on the o-train."
- **paper jam:** the paper gets stuck between two desks. click it 10 times to clear it. "pc load letter."
- **fines:** you get fined for things as you use the site. typing too fast, hovering too long, using a question mark. they are added to the processing fee.
- **come back:** when you switch tabs, the tab title becomes "your case is unattended" and the icon becomes a red stamp.
- **peel the stickers:** stickers peel off when clicked and leave residue. peeling "property of the department" adds a fine.
- **department merger:** mid-case, two departments merge into something like "the bureau of snack gravity", and both clerks squeeze behind one desk.
- **clerk sneeze:** a clerk sneezes and the paper blows back one desk.
- **invented holidays:** some dates are national holidays, like "the day of the stapler". all departments are closed.
- **follow-up question:** a department asks "why do you need to blink?". any answer is "insufficient". good for the claude version later.
- **2fa:** "we've sent a verification code to your fax machine."
- **draft autosave:** "draft saved", then a second later "draft rejected".

## codex brief: pitch slideshow

**task:** build a short pitch slideshow (8 to 10 slides) for this project, to present at the hackathon. use ai-generated images for the visuals.

**the joke the slideshow plays:** the pitch is delivered completely straight, as if this were a serious government service. the deadpan is the comedy. the product is silly, and the deck should not be.

### visual style

- **photos look real.** generate photorealistic, slightly dull, fluorescent-lit government office photography: beige cubicles, filing cabinets, stacks of forms, wall clocks, laminated signs, rubber stamps, paper trays, water-cooler corners. think "stock photo of a municipal office," shot on a mid-range camera, flat lighting, slightly desaturated. no fantasy, no cartoon look, no obvious ai gloss.
- **match the app.** the app looks like a dull government website (cream grainy background, navy, manila folders, stamp red) with a small pixel-art office in the middle. use real app screenshots next to the photos, so the tiny pixel clerks against real office photos is part of the joke.
- **palette:** cream `#f5efe1`, paper `#fdfaf2`, navy `#1c2b4a`, manila `#e9d8a6`, stamp red `#c8312f`, approved green `#2e7d4f`, ink `#1f2430`.
- **type:** ibm plex serif for titles, ibm plex sans for body text, ibm plex mono for labels and case numbers.
- **layout:** one idea per slide, a large image, and at most two short lines of text, all lowercase to match the app. no bullet walls.
- **image consistency:** keep the same office, lighting and colour grade across all generated images so they feel like one photo shoot. reuse one base prompt and vary only the subject.

### suggested slides

1. **title:** "department of useless affairs." image: the exterior of a drab government building with a small sign. subtitle: "a service nobody asked for."
2. **the problem:** "citizens are doing things without permission." image: a person blinking at a desk, looking unsure. line: "every day, millions of people blink. unsupervised."
3. **the solution:** "every request gets its own departments. most say no." image: a corridor of identical numbered office doors.
4. **meet the departments:** one office photo with seven small labelled callouts. show that they change per request, e.g. "can i not submit my homework?" goes to the office of the dog that ate it and the department of not doing things.
5. **live demo:** a real screenshot of the app mid-request. cut to the app live here.
6. **the certificate:** a photo of a framed certificate on an office wall, with the app's real certificate beside it. line: "valid for 4 minutes. void if enjoyed."
7. **how it works (briefly):** "ai reads your request and assembles the departments most likely to say no. zero dependencies. zero purpose." image: a server room or a filing cabinet labelled "ai."
8. **testimonials (fake, clearly absurd):** "i blinked with permission for the first time." a stock-photo-style portrait of a smiling office worker. label them as fictional in small print.
9. **roadmap:** "q1: reject you twice. q2: a form for the form." image: a very tall stack of paper.
10. **thank you:** "your request has been received. please wait 6 to 8 business years." image: an empty waiting room with a ticket dispenser.

### image generation prompts

use this base for every photo and swap in the subject:

> photorealistic photo of {subject}, inside a drab municipal government office, fluorescent ceiling lighting, beige walls, slightly desaturated colour grade, shot on a mid-range dslr, 35mm, shallow depth of field, mundane and bureaucratic mood, no text unless specified, no people unless specified.

example subjects:

- a long corridor with seven identical numbered office doors
- a desk with a rubber stamp, an ink pad and a tall stack of forms
- a wall clock above a filing cabinet, with a handwritten "back in 5 min" note
- a laminated sign reading "please take a number" next to a ticket dispenser
- a framed certificate on a beige wall beside a dying office plant
- an empty waiting room with plastic chairs bolted together

### rules

- do not use real logos, real government crests or seals, or real people's likenesses. invent a generic crest if one is needed.
- label the testimonial people as fictional.
- keep the deck readable on a projector: large type, high contrast.
- deliver as a self-contained deck (html or pptx) with images in an `assets/` folder, plus the list of prompts used for each image.
- take app screenshots from the running app using `?fast&req=...` rather than mocking them up.
